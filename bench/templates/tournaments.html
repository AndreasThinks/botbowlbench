Tournaments

A round robin pits every model against every other one. A placement is scheduled automatically whenever a new model joins: the newcomer plays five anchors spread across the table, then a few top-up games against the opponents closest to it. Newcomers that haven't started yet share one placement cohort: their games are interleaved, a game they have in common is played once and counts for both, and each keeps its own anchors and top-up allowance. Older gauntlets had newcomers play everyone.
